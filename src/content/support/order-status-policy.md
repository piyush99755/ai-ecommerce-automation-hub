# Order Status Explanations & Lifecycle Policy

Understanding Your Order Status:
- PENDING: Your order has been received and is awaiting payment processing or initial fulfillment queueing.
- PROCESSING: Payment is confirmed and items are being picked, packed, and prepared for shipping.
- SHIPPED: Your package has been handed over to the delivery carrier and is in transit. Tracking info is available.
- DELIVERED: The carrier has confirmed delivery to the destination address.
- ON_HOLD: Your order requires additional verification or stock reservation. Contact support for assistance.
- CANCELLED: The order has been cancelled and will not be processed further.
