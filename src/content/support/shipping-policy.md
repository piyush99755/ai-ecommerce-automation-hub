# Store Shipping & Delivery Policy

Demo Store Shipping Terms & Estimates:
- Standard Shipping: Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days across domestic regions.
- Expedited Shipping: Next-day or 2-day expedited shipping is available at checkout.
- Order Tracking: Once an order status transitions to SHIPPED, a carrier tracking number and carrier name (e.g. UPS, FedEx, USPS) are assigned to your order.
- Delivery Confirmation: The order status is updated to DELIVERED once the carrier confirms final doorstep delivery.
- Shipping Charges: Calculated dynamically during Stripe Checkout based on items and delivery address.
